A product & brand designer who likes being close to the problem.

Clarity from Complexity

Most of my career has been spent solving design problems that don't have a clean answer: complex products, multi-platform systems, identities that need to scale without losing what made them interesting in the first place. The messier the problem, the more there is to find.

Consideration at Any Scale

Brand and systems work is where I live. The design systems I've built for enterprise software are made for experts, people who need the interface to get out of their way and have strong opinions about it. I've also worked with founders at the very beginning, when the brand is still just a vibe and a font they found on Pinterest. Whatever the scale, the same level of consideration goes into the work. No client gets the lazy version.

Agency & In-House Experience

Time in-house at large tech companies taught me how design decisions compound over time, how one bad choice in 2019 is still haunting someone's component library today. A consultancy background means fast, polished, and right the first time, because there is no second time. I carry both, like a well-organized tote bag.

Partners, Not Vendors

Design doesn't happen in isolation. The more context there is, about your business, your users, the thing nobody put in the brief but everyone already knows, the better the work gets. I'm at my best when I'm close to the problem and the people trying to solve it.
